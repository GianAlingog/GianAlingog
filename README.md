# Hi, I'm Gian

CS student at UC Santa Barbara, expected to graduate by June 2028. I like problems where correctness and speed matter, which is how I got into competitive programming, and lately into systems and performance work.

## What I'm building

**[Lion](https://github.com/GianAlingog/lion)**: A modern Tetris engine and bot in Rust.
Zero-dependency engine (bitboards, SRS with kick tables, spin detection), BFS move
generation, and a beam search over the Dellacherie feature set. Median 102 pieces per
40-line sprint, against a theoretical optimum of 100. Versus mode in progress.

**Feli** *(private)*: Internal task management for the UCSB CS department, live with
26 faculty and staff. I build and maintain it as a research assistant under
Prof. Maryam Majedi.

**[Compr](https://github.com/GianAlingog/compr)**: File compressor in C++. Huffman
coding over a custom archive format.

**[Forcr](https://github.com/GianAlingog/forcr)**: Codeforces training tool: practice
tracking and rating analysis.

**[IRL Noteblock](https://github.com/GianAlingog/irl-noteblock)**: Minecraft noteblocks,
in real life. SB Hacks 2026.

**[Blog](https://gianalingog.github.io/)**: Occasional writing, mostly algorithms.
